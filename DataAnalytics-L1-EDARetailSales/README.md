# 📖 Project Overview

This project performs an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a Retail Sales Dataset using Python. The objective is to understand customer purchasing behavior, identify sales trends, discover business insights, and visualize important patterns through descriptive statistics and graphical analysis.

---

# 🎯 Objective

The main objective of this project is to:

- Understand the structure of the retail sales dataset.
- Perform data cleaning and preprocessing.
- Generate descriptive statistics.
- Analyze monthly and quarterly sales trends.
- Study customer demographics.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Discover relationships between numerical variables using correlation analysis.
- Generate business insights and recommendations.

---

# 📂 Dataset Information

The dataset contains retail transaction records with the following columns:

- Transaction ID
- Date
- Customer ID
- Gender
- Age
- Product Category
- Quantity
- Price per Unit
- Total Amount

Additional columns created during analysis:

- Month
- Quarter

# 📌 Key Findings

- Sales varied significantly throughout the year.
- Quarter 4 generated the highest overall revenue.
- Electronics generated the highest total revenue.
- Male and female customer counts were nearly equal.
- Customers aged 46–55 represented the largest age group.
- Price per Unit showed a strong positive correlation with Total Amount.
- Quantity had a moderate positive correlation with Total Amount.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

- Increase inventory before Quarter 4 due to higher demand.
- Promote Electronics products through targeted marketing campaigns.
- Focus marketing strategies on the 46–55 age group.
- Maintain balanced marketing efforts for both male and female customers.
- Introduce seasonal promotions during lower-performing months.
